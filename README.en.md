## About Me

I am a software engineer from Taiwan. I enjoy deep learning and AI, and I like to understand how technology works at a low level. My current interests are:

- **Computer vision and image processing**: I write basic image-processing algorithms from scratch in C, so they can run on edge devices with limited computing resources, such as the Raspberry Pi. I also want to turn these algorithms into an easy-to-use library that developers can compile directly into their own projects.
- **Deep learning**: I train and compress small models with PyTorch. I also have experience deploying models to edge devices with TensorRT, ONNX, and NCNN.
- **AI agents**: I build features such as tool calling and permission control, and connect them to local or cloud-hosted large language models. I am still improving my skills in this area.
- **Personal tools and portfolio**: I build small web apps that I actually use in daily life.
